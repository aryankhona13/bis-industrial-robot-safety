Frequently Asked Questions

Does BIS certify every industrial robot?

No. BIS develops standards; mandatory requirements depend on specific laws and schemes.

Why start with hazards?

Because safety design begins with understanding what can cause harm.

Why are sensors not enough?

Sensors only detect; you need control logic and actuation for safety.

Why is testing shown in the flow?

Verification provides proof that the safety behavior works as intended.
