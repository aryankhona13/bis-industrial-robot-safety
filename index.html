01 • THE FOUNDATION

Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

India’s National Standards Body

BIS develops Indian Standards for products, processes and services.

Safety Quality Reliability Performance

BIS works through areas such as standards, testing, certification, conformity assessment and consumer protection.

BIS in Engineering

Modern engineering systems contain many interconnected parts. Standards help engineers work toward safety, reliability, and performance.

04 • THE MAIN EXAMPLE

Industrial Robots

Powerful automation needs strong safety.

An industrial robot is a programmable machine used to perform manufacturing tasks automatically.

Welding Assembly Handling Inspection
Automatic movement, high force and unexpected motion can create hazards such as collision, crushing, and impact.
